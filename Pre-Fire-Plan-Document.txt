================================================================================
Flammable liquid, toxic by inhalation (at 1000 ppm can cause unconsciousness
or death), skin irritant, Boil over.

================================================================================
OPERATIONS RESPONSE
================================================================================

  • Roof drain to be closed immediately, if possible. Closing of Dyke Valve
    shall be done to ensure containment of oil inside dyke area.

  • Operation such as receipt/dispense/transfer should be stopped if operation
    is going on. However, emptying out of tank can be done as per consultation
    with senior operation personnel.

  • Immediately tank should be isolated in tank manifold and observe the tank
    for any other abnormalities.

  • Inform Fire & Safety at 333/444/3576 or VHF channel 8 or by breaking
    nearby MCP.

  • The tank information (Tank Level, Temperature, Transfer status etc.) shall
    be shared to SIC (F&S) upon their arrival. Roof drain valve, if not closed
    shall be conveyed to SIC (F&S) to handle the emergency accordingly.

  • Cooling water isolation valve to be opened for cooling purpose if it found
    necessary. Cooling of adjacent tanks through water monitors is not
    recommended. However, water shielding from heat radiation can be done from
    monitors, if required.

  • Product transfer to be started from the affected tank to another tank if
    sufficient ullage is available in the other tank.

  Post Fire Extinguishment
  ------------------------
  • Tank to be emptied out as soon as possible and accumulated oil in the roof
    to be safely evacuated.
  • Tank to be handed over to Inspection and Maintenance for necessary
    rectification of the roof and inspection of tank.

================================================================================
TACTICAL EMERGENCY RESPONSE
================================================================================

  1st Turnout Duty:
  -----------------
  • Fire tender is parked close to hydrant 10/H-28 along Road no. 10.
    (Within 3 min)

  • Two 15m hose connected from hydrant 10/H-28 to fire tender. One nearby
    Hydrant (10/H-27) shall be used for providing foam to HVLRM through JRCP.
    JRCP placement can be done over the foam tank on fire tender for prompt
    and easy application of foam.

  • 1 AMC will open the isolation valves for water spray system for adjacent
    tank (101B). Monitoring of tank's temperature will be done and after
    significant reduction, decision on closing of valve can be taken based on
    FW availability, FW network pressure, flame intensity on burning tank,
    temperature of exposed tank etc.

  • 2 TA will connect 15m hose through JRCP to HVLRM 10/12 to supply
    finished foam on the top of the tank.

  • SIC will contact control room for 2nd turnout along with HEFG & Trolley
    mounted foam monitor. (After 3 min from arrival of site). HEFG
    requirement is for dyke spillage and fire cases.

  After the arrival of 2nd Turnout:
  ----------------------------------
  • Park fire tender close to hydrant 12/H-20 along road no. 12.

  • Two 15m hose connected from hydrant 12/H-20 to fire tender.

  • With the help of MEFG (at road no 10&12) finished foam is supplied on
    tank dyke area in case of dyke spillage/fire.

  • 2 TA will connect 15m hose through JRCP to HVLRM 12/04 to supply
    finished foam on the top of the tank.

  • 1 AMC will open the isolation valves for water spray system for adjacent
    tank (101B). Continuous foam application can be done from Fire Tender
    also, if the throw & range is sufficient.

  • SIC will contact control room for sending foam nurser. (After arrival of
    2nd turnout). Continuous foam application can be done from Fire Tender
    also, if the throw & range is sufficient.

  Urgent Evacuation Condition:
  ----------------------------
  • As advised by CEC/IC, mainly during pre-indication stage of Boil Over
    which can be detected by change of Tank shell's paint and increase in
    flames.

PROTECTION OF ADJACENT EQUIPMENT
================================================================================

  40-TT-FR-101B

  • From road no 10 & 12, open isolation valve for Water spray system for
    even cooling and shielding from radiation.

  • Ensure foam flooding in rim seal area of adjacent Floating roof tank
    TT-FR-101B.

  In case, wind direction is from north to south we have to use water spray
  system on the following tanks:
    • 44-TT-FR-104C (isolation valve on East side of Rd no.15 & South side
      of Rd no.10 have to be open by 2nd turnout AMC)
    • 44-TT-FR-102A (isolation valve on West side of Rd no.15 & South side
      of Rd no.10 have to be open by 2nd turnout AMC).

  The above-mentioned plan is subjected to FW network pressure and Fire Water
  availability. Otherwise, preference for cooling will be given to storage
  tanks of lighter products, small storage tanks and empty storage tanks.
